# Service HQ Website Plan

## Goal
Create a clean, trustworthy lead-generation website for Service HQ Inc. using only provided business information.

## Pages
- Home
- About
- Services
- Air Conditioning Repair
- Heating Services
- Appliance Repair
- Reviews
- Areas Served
- Broward County
- Palm Beach County
- Miami-Dade County
- Contact

## Rules
- Do not invent business details.
- Omit missing license, hours, financing, warranties, and city-specific claims until provided.
- Use the provided phone, email, address, service counties, Google review link, and company description.
